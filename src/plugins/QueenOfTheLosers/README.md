# Queen of the Losers

Allows Configuration of the \"King of the Losers\" screen to show other titles, such as Queen / Ruler. As well as dead voice & TTS pitch options.

All settings are per-player and synced to other players with the mod.

Use the [REPOConfig](https://thunderstore.io/c/repo/p/nickklmao/REPOConfig/) mod to change settings while in game.

## Feature List:
- Title Changes (Queen, Ruler, King, Monarch)
- Death head pitch and low battery inflection
- TTS pitch adjustment

## Multiplayer

- If you have the mod and the host does not, only you will see the effects of your changes.
- If you and the host have the mod, everyone else with the mod will also see the effects of your changes.
- If anyone else does not have the mod, they will not see your changes.

unfortunately it appears all voice effects are done on the recieving clients, which is why I could not make the voice effects client side only.
